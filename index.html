Features

Only the people
who were there

Every Momento album has a sharing zone. Guests can join only while they’re inside it, so a wedding album never fills with strangers’ photos.

150 m

4 of 9 guests can join

Out of range? Momento tells guests how far to move, and lets them know when they’re close enough. Momento Plus unlocks bigger zones.

Cameras and phones, together

Sony ARW, Canon CR3, iPhone HEIC and ProRAW all land as untouched originals.

Uploads that behave

Camera files go first. On mobile data uploads can wait for Wi-Fi, and offline shares pick up on their own.

Your cloud, your copy

Photos save to Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox or OneDrive. Nothing gets locked inside Momento.

Sorted for you

Each file’s camera data groups the album by device. Filter by phones, cameras or videos.
